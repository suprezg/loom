2. COMMERCIAL USE, NON-CONTAMINATION & SAFELISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Loom Project is designed from the ground up to be safe and frictionless for 
commercial, proprietary, and enterprise software engineering:

(a) Non-Infection of User Specifications:
    Writing, maintaining, or compiling specification files (.spec.md) using Loom 
    does NOT subject your specifications, architecture, or proprietary ideas to any 
    copyleft obligations. Your specifications belong entirely to you.

(b) Non-Infection of Generated Code:
    Source code, boilerplate, database migrations, tests, and artifacts generated 
    by Loom or Loom Yarn plugins are the sole property of the user or organization 
    running the tool. The Loom compiler asserts no copyright or intellectual 
    property ownership over generated output.

(c) Non-Infection of Custom Plugins (Yarns):
    Yarns and plugins communicating with Loom via standard JSON AST, IPC, or CLI 
    interfaces are considered independent works. Organizations and developers are 
    free to author, distribute, and commercialize proprietary Yarns without 
    disclosing source code or altering their own licensing terms.

(d) Safe for Enterprise CI/CD Pipelines:
    Because the compiler is licensed under MPL 2.0 rather than GNU GPL/AGPL, 
    enterprise development teams can incorporate the Loom binary into internal 
    continuous integration pipelines without risking copyleft contagion across 
    proprietary company repositories.
